This is a digest of the bonus footage from the first press limited edition (Light Music Edition) of RE-GE's 2nd single, "I'll Always Find You No Matter Where You Are." The challenge, titled "Reach the Reward with Bonds! Re-Ge Walk," tests the members' ability to locate a specific store using only its name, without any smartphone assistance. Divided into two teams, they embark on this quest, highlighting their teamwork and problem-solving skills. The video features members from the newly formed girls' group RE-GE, a collaboration between Stardust Promotion and Bandai Namco Music Live.
