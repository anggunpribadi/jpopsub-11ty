In this special episode of **Ebichu++ (たすたす)**, the beloved idol group **Shiritsu Ebisu Chugaku (Ebichu)** pulls off an unforgettable surprise for two of its members, Ayaka Yasumoto and Kaho Kobayashi, both celebrating their birthdays in June. The episode kicks off with a covert mission: the rest of the Ebichu members secretly plot and prepare a handmade, heartwarming birthday party for Yasumoto and Kobayashi—without them knowing. As the team brainstorms wildly creative and unpredictable surprise ideas, the hosts and comedians are left entertained and speechless.

When the birthday girls arrive at the venue, unexpected twists unfold: tensions rise amid the surprise, sparking some tears and laughter. Will their efforts to express heartfelt feelings and friendship through this unique party succeed? Alongside the main event, the episode also introduces a new development in the Ebichu Game Project, inviting production staff and members to collaborate on a fresh gaming concept. Viewers get an exclusive look behind the scenes as the mysterious game slowly takes shape.

Join this emotionally charged and entertaining episode to witness candid reactions, unforgettable surprises, and the genuine bonds within Ebichu. Whether you’re a longtime fan or new to the group, this episode promises laughter, tears, and lots of fun.
