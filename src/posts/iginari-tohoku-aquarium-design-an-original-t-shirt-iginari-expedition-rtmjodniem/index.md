This time, members of the Iginari expedition will be taking on the challenge of designing T-shirts to be worn at the "Iginari Tohoku Aquarium" held on 4/20 and 4/21 by members Uka Yasuto and Pearl Kichise!

“Iginari Tohoku Aquarium @Sendai Umino-Mori Aquarium 2024” will be held!

On Saturday, April 20th, "Iginari from Tohoku" will experience the work of a zookeeper at Sendai Umino-Mori Aquarium!

In the afternoon session, dolphins, sea lions, and birds will perform "STADIUM LIVE", an original performance using songs from Iginari Tohoku, and you will also experience the work of zookeepers in various places in the museum!

In the evening, a quiz competition with customer participation will be held at Uminomori Stadium!
Chance to get gorgeous prizes!


On April 21st (Sunday), we will be holding a penguin visit event, Iginari Tohoku mini live and special event at EBeans in front of Sendai Station!

*Depending on the situation, the penguin visit event may be canceled.
