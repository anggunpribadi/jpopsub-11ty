In this episode of **Airiko Radio** (#5), Kiyama Ai and Ueda Riko—members of the popular Kyushu idol group *Batten Shoujo Tai*—share their candid thoughts as they enter the rainy season. Broadcasting on a rainy June day, the pair chat about everything from their favorite English words and the sounds that inspire them, to humorous stories of braving a typhoon, and how to (mis)handle an umbrella in unpredictable weather!

This episode marks the first time Ai and Riko openly discuss the English words they love and reflect on how these new expressions influence them personally. The conversation also takes a light-hearted turn as the hosts imagine what they’d want to experience if they could be reborn.

**Airiko Radio** is the weekly show where Ai and Riko offer warmth and relatability, perfect for rainy days or cozy nights in. Join these Batten Shoujo Tai idols as they share laughter, personal growth, and friendly advice—rain or shine.

**Tune in every Wednesday at 23:30 JST for your weekly dose of Airiko Radio!**
