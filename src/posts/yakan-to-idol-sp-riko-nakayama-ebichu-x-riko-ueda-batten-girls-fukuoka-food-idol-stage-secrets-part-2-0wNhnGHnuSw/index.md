Join us for the highly anticipated second part of the "Riko Riko Interview" on Yakan to Idol SP, featuring Riko Nakayama from Shiritsu Ebisu Chugaku and Riko Ueda from Batten ShoujoTai! This special collaboration finally brings together two popular idols with the same first name for an insightful and heartwarming conversation.

In this exciting continuation, the two idols delve into a fascinating exchange of questions, revealing more about their personal lives and experiences. Riko Ueda kicks things off by asking Riko Nakayama for her top recommendations for post-live show dining in Fukuoka, leading to exciting insights into local delicacies like Motsunabe, the unique flavor of Yuzukosho, and famous ramen spots like Shinn-Shin. They even hint at a future meet-up over delicious food and drinks, discussing their approaches to alcohol now that some members are over 20!

Then, Riko Nakayama turns the tables, inquiring about Riko Ueda's strategies for overcoming pre-stage nervousness. Discover their personal routines and tips for managing anxiety before a big performance, including Ueda Riko's "meditation time" and "Nakayama Time" before Ebichu lives. Don't miss this unique opportunity to see the two Rikos connect, share personal anecdotes, and build a heartwarming friendship.

"Yakan to Idol" brings you closer to your favorite idols as a kettle with a personality brews delicious tea and sparks captivating conversations. Tune in for new episodes every weekday at 7 PM JST and weekends/holidays at 12 PM JST!
