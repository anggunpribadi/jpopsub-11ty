In this episode, Hoshina making a return visit to the lively Sancha (Sangenjaya) district, specifically to the wonderful "Sancha Mare" restaurant. But why the revisit? 

As her kick off a lovely spring evening, still bright and early at 5 PM, perfect for some daytime drinking! She'll share snippets of her busy schedule leading up to this evening, including acupuncture and an event. We'll dive into the extensive sake menu, savor some crispy bell peppers ("paripari piman"), and enjoy other delightful Japanese dishes. She also discuss her preference for daytime drinking over late nights due to getting sleepy! And, of course, the main event: She'll finally reveal the reason behind her newfound love for spicy food, all while sipping on some exquisite nihonshu.
