### Episode Summary
Welcome back to *Yuzuki no Heya* (Yuzuki's Room)! In this episode, our hosts Yuzuki Oguro and Shunsuke Setoguchi kick things off with a lively discussion on how to beat the dreaded "May Blues" (May depression) before diving into a jam-packed show.

We are thrilled to welcome two special guests from **Shiritsu Ebisu Chugaku**: **Nonoka Kazami** and **Emma Sakurai**. It’s a milestone episode for the show, as Shunsuke finally completes his "mission" of having met every member of the group on the show! 

**In this episode, we cover:**
*   **Special Guest Interview:** A deep dive conversation with Nonoka Kazami and Emma Sakurai about their recent activities and their experience on *Yuzuki no Heya*.
*   **LumiUnion Update:** The hosts share their recap and highlights from attending the recent LumiUnion last tour—complete with exclusive video footage!
*   **The May Blues Cure:** Tips and banter on how to stay positive as we head into the month of May.
