Join Batten Shoujotai members Kiyama Ai and Ueda Riko for the second episode of their weekly podcast, "AiiRiko Radio"! This time, the two explore their long friendship, reminiscing about the first time they met and sharing their initial impressions of each other. With candid stories that take you on a nostalgic time slip back to their childhood days as elementary and junior high school students, the duo laughs about their earliest memories—right down to the details of how they sat on chairs!

Having known each other for over a decade, Ai and Riko reflect on their journey together in the idol world and their personal growth. The conversation is full of heartfelt moments, behind-the-scenes tidbits, and warm humor that longtime fans and newcomers alike will enjoy. 

Tune in for a uniquely personal chat that celebrates friendship, youth, and the enduring bond between two of Kyushu’s favorite idols. Don’t miss this fun trip down memory lane with “AiiRiko Radio”!
