Experience the inspiring journey of MISS MERCY in "Be REBORN," the second installment of their documentary series. This exclusive behind-the-scenes film follows the seven-member girl group as they strive toward their major debut, showcasing the passion, perseverance, and teamwork that define their story.

From intense vocal and dance rehearsals to candid moments of camaraderie, fans witness the dedication that has carried MISS MERCY from their first days to the threshold of mainstream success. Highlighting challenges and triumphs, the documentary captures the group’s emotional preparation—including setbacks, setlist discussions, and performances—showing that only those who never give up can become stars.

Meet MIYU, SARA, SHUKA, ION, YUKI, KAREN, and COCONA, collectively known as "Marcy," as they reflect on their growth, personal aspirations, and the power of unity. With their major 1st single set for release on October 14, 2026, and an exciting lineup of live events and special appearances, MISS MERCY is ready to redefine what it means to be cute and cool.
