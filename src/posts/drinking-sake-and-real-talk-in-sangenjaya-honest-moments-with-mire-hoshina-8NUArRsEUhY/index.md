Join Mirei Hoshina as she launches her long-awaited YouTube channel "Hoshina no Moto" with a heartfelt and relaxed visit to one of her favorite spots in Sangenjaya. In this debut episode, Mirei—who adores Japanese sake and delicious food—takes viewers to the legendary sake bar "Akaoni," renowned as a true haven for nihonshu lovers. 

Over drinks, Mirei candidly discusses her journey into YouTube, her passion for Japanese sake, and some untold stories from her personal life and career. Immerse yourself in the cozy, authentic local izakaya atmosphere as She samples unique sake brands, shares tasting notes, and enjoys simple yet mouthwatering Japanese dishes like shirohaku (a fruity nihonshu) and fresh onions.
