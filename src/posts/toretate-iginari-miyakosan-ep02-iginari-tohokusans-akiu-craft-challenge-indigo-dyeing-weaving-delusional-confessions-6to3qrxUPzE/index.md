This is the second episode of "Toretate! Iginari Miyakou-san!", a new program presented by the idol group Iginari Tohoku-san and Miyagi Kotsu. Following their previous adventure into traditional crafts, the members embark on another exciting challenge in the historic Akiu Craft Village. This time, they visit the Senshoku Tsuru Kobo Tsumugi workshop to experience the intricate art of indigo dyeing for handkerchiefs and traditional hand-loom weaving to create unique coasters.

Join the members as they immerse themselves in these age-old techniques, navigating the crafting process with laughter, surprises, and personal touches that make each creation truly one-of-a-kind. The episode embodies the show's theme of "challenge," celebrating the spirit of endeavor in Tohoku. As a special bonus, don't miss the "Delusional Confession Theater," a segment where the members' wildest romantic fantasies explode into comedic confessions. This series aligns with Miyagi Kotsu's brand message, "Carrying Challenges," highlighting those who strive in the Tohoku region and the value of public transportation in supporting their journey.
