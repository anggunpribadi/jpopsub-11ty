This video features Ruri Shiraishi from the new girl group RE-GE (リージェ) participating in a "100 Questions, 100 Answers" segment. Get to know Ruri better as she answers a variety of questions about herself, from her personal details like birthday and blood type to her hobbies, personality, and even her favorite foods. This is a great opportunity for fans to discover more about one of RE-GE's talented members.


**About RE-GE:**  
RE-GE is a new girl group formed by Stardust Promotion and Bandai Namco Music Live. With the catchphrase "The moment you're curious, that's the beginning. Support yourself, and begin the future," the group was formed through the "HAJIMARE Project" audition. They debuted on February 15, 2026, with their first single "0秒前" on June 3rd of the same year.
