The five young members of the idol group Ebisu Junior High School, also known as the "low grade members," have released their first photo book depicting their "youth from school days." Member Kokubo revealed the appeal of the photo book, which contains many natural shots, saying, "I think you'll get the illusion that these five people were in the same class."

Member Nonoka Kazami said, "I was worried that I wouldn't be able to take cute photos because it was too busy, but I think we were able to create a photo book that only the five of us could create." She was confident in her completion.

Furthermore, regarding the future goals of Shiritsu Ebisu Junior High School, which will celebrate its 15th anniversary this year, Kokona Sakuragi said, "As a group, our goal was to perform at the Saitama Super Arena, but it was decided that the performance would take place in 2025. But I don't think that's the goal, I want more people to know about it, and I want more people to discover it.And I want to aim to be at Tokyo Dome, so I'm not going to stop! I want to show you that," she declared forcefully.

This photobook features five "lower grade members" who joined Ebichu from 2021: Kokona Sakuragi, Yuno Kokubo, Nonoka Kazami, Ema Sakurai, and Yuna Nakamura.
