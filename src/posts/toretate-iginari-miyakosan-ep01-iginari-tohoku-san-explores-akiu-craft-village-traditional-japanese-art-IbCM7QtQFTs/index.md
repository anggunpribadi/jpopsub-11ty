This is the first episode of "Toretate! Iginari Miyakosan!", a new collaborative program featuring idol group Iginari Tohoku-san and Miyagi Kotsu. In this episode, the members travel by bus to Akiu Craft Village in Sendai, Miyagi Prefecture, to explore traditional Japanese crafts. They learn about the techniques and passion of local artisans who preserve traditional skills while also taking on new challenges. The program's core theme is "challenge," and the members of Iginari Tohoku-san engage in various discoveries and personal trials throughout their journey. The video also highlights Miyagi Kotsu's brand message, "Carrying Challenges," which supports individuals and regions in Tohoku, and features information on their recruitment efforts for bus drivers and mechanics.
