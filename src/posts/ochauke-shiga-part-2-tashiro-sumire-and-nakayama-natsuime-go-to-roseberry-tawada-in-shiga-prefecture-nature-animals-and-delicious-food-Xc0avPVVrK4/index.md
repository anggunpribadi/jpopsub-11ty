OCHA NORMA

It is a coined word that combines the word "Ocha Norma" (a place where everyone gathers and relax) and the word "NORMA" (norm), which means "standard" in Latin.

We hope that they will become a new generation standard that entertains the living room! We named them with the hope that they will continue to do so in the future.

We will use the diverse characters of our members to deliver performances and content that can be enjoyed by people of all ages and genders.
