🌹What is Rosie Chronicle

"Rosy" means "rose-colored" and "full of hope" and "full of happiness",

and "Chronicle" means "chronicle", a historical book that records events by year.

The name was chosen with the hope that they would weave a rose-colored history that would be passed down forever.
