**Welcome back to *Yuzuki's Room*!** In this **Season 2, Episode 12**, hosts Oguro Yuzuki and Setoguchi Shunsuke are joined by two special guests from the popular group **Shiritsu Ebisu Chugaku (Ebichu)**: **Konan Sakuraki** and **Yunoko Kokubo**!  

### **What’s in this episode?**  
- A fun and relaxed chat with **Konan Sakuraki**, who previously appeared in a surprise segment, reminiscing about her love for *horumon* (offal)!  
- **Yunoko Kokubo’s** first appearance on the show, sharing her connection with Oguro Yuzuki—turns out they’re from the same hometown!  
- Behind-the-scenes stories, lighthearted banter, and a peek into their recent activities, including upcoming stage performances.  

Tune in for an entertaining and heartwarming conversation between idols, packed with laughter and unexpected revelations!
