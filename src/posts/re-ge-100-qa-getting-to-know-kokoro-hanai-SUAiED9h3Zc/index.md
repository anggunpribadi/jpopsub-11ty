### Video Summary
In this installment of the RE-GE "100 Questions & 100 Answers" series, member Kokoro Hanai sits down for a rapid-fire interview to share more about her personality, hobbies, and hidden talents. 

During the session, Kokoro answers questions ranging from her birthday and MBTI type (INFP) to her unique ability to move her ears. She also shares personal insights, such as her love for gaming, her fondness for bringing her stuffed "Piyo" plushie on outings, and her life at home with her pet parakeet. The conversation is facilitated by fellow member Yuumi Hiyama, providing fans with a deeper, personal look at one of the newest voices in the J-pop scene.

### About RE-GE
RE-GE (リージェ) is a Japanese girl group formed through the "HAJIMARE Project," a collaboration between Stardust Promotion and Bandai Namco Music Live. Debuting in February 2026 with their major single "0-byou mae" (0 seconds before), the group consists of nine members: Ruri Shiraishi, Jui Nogimoto, Kokoro Hanai, Riri Takatsuki, Anna Tasaki, Kokoro Morise, Aoi Mizukawa, Yuumi Hiyama, and Hohomi Nasu.
