In this video, Aoi Mizukawa, a member of the newly debuted girl group RE-GE, takes on the "100 Questions, 100 Answers" challenge. This segment provides fans with an in-depth look at her personality, preferences, and personal life. 

During the session, Aoi answers a wide variety of questions—ranging from her favorite foods (sun-dried snacks and ramen) and hobbies (watching movies and visiting ramen shops) to her childhood dreams and personality traits (described as laid-back and easygoing). This intimate Q&A session serves as an opportunity for fans to learn more about the members behind the "HAJIMARE Project" as the group embarks on their first summer since their major debut in June 2026.

**About RE-GE:**
Formed through the "HAJIMARE Project," a collaboration between Stardust Promotion and Bandai Namco Music Live, RE-GE is a nine-member girl group that debuted on February 15, 2026. Their major debut single, "0-byou mae" (0 Seconds Before), was released on June 3, 2026.
