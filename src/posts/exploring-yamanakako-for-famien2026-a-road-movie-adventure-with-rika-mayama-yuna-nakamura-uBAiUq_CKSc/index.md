Join Rika Mayama and Yuna Nakamura from Shiritsu Ebisu Chugaku (Ebichu) as they journey to Yamanakako for the annual "FAMIEN2026" music festival! In this road movie-style vlog, the duo sets out early in the morning, sharing their travel experience by both car and train, with plenty of lively banter and travel tips along the way.

Perfect for viewers considering attending FAMIEN2026 or anyone curious about getting to Yamanakako, the video highlights all the important details—from official transportation options like the JTB Official Bus Tour to alternative methods. Mayama and Yuna  discuss festival themes ("EMO-chuu" and "Limit Break") and stress that the adventure begins not just at the venue, but from the very moment you start your journey.
