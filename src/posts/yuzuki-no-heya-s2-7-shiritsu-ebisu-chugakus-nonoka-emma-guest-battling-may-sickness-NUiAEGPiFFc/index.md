Welcome to "Yuzuki no Heya" (Yuzuki's Room) Season 2, Episode 7! Join hosts Oguro Yuzuki and Setoguchi Shunsuke in their peaceful bi-weekly program as they welcome two very special guests: Kazami Nonoka and Sakurai Emma from the popular idol group Shiritsu Ebisu Chugaku!

In this heartwarming episode, the quartet dives into an engaging discussion about the impending "May Sickness" (Gogatsu-byou), sharing thoughts and laughter to help viewers blow away any end-of-April anxieties. Witness a truly special moment as Sakurai Emma makes her highly anticipated first appearance on "Yuzuki no Heya," much to the delight of Setoguchi Shunsuke, who humorously declares his personal mission to meet all Ebichu members finally complete! Kazami Nonoka, a returning guest, adds to the lively atmosphere with her familiar charm.

Get ready for an episode packed with fun conversations, genuine interactions, and a dose of positive energy designed to brighten your day and chase away the May blues!
