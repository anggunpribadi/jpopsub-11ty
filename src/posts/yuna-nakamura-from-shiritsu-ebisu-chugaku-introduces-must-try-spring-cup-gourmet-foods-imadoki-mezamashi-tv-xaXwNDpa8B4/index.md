Join Yuna Nakamura from Shiritsu Ebisu Chugaku as she embarks on a delicious spring adventure, introducing the latest trendy "cup gourmet" foods perfect for your next outing. Featured on Fuji TV’s "Mezamashi TV" in its popular "Imadoki" segment, Yuna takes viewers on a vibrant tour of Tokyo’s top sweet and savory destinations, sharing her personal tasting notes and recommendations.

Highlights include:
- The newly released Harajuku Waffle Cups, made with eggs for a rich, fluffy texture and luscious, high-fat cream.
- Mammy’s Un Sourire’s bite-sized Petit Apple Pies, featuring extra crispy pastry and melt-in-your-mouth apple compote.
- The buzz-worthy Xiao Mala in Shimokitazawa, where customers select from skewered dumplings and shrimp, fried crispy and tossed in spicy mala oil and spices for an addictive heat.
- Bell Frites in Kichijoji, serving authentic, twice-fried Hokkaido potatoes that are crispy on the outside and fluffy inside—with a variety of flavors and popular truffle mayo topping.

Whether you’re a foodie, a spring explorer, or a fan of Yuna and Ebichu, this episode delivers mouthwatering reviews, fun food commentary, and perfect takeout ideas for your next trip around Tokyo.
