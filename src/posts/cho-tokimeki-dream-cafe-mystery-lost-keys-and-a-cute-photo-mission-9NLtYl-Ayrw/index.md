Join the members of Chō Tokimeki♡Sendenbu (Super Tokimeki♡Dream) as they visit the special collaboration cafe, "Chō Tokimeki♡Dream Cafe." Upon arrival, a crisis unfolds: the chef has lost the key to the kitchen, meaning the delicious menu items are unavailable. To resolve this, the group embarks on a mission to find the missing key. Their quest leads them to a puzzle involving life-sized standees of "Paburin," where they must perfectly replicate poses for a fun photo challenge to help unlock the kitchen and enjoy the cafe's offerings.
