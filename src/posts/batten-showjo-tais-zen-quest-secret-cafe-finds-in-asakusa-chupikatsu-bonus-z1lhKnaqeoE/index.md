This bonus episode of "ChupiKatsu" features J-Pop idol group Batten Showjo Tai. Initially setting out for a Zazen experience to suppress worldly desires, the group's adventure also includes exploring a unique part of Tokyo's Asakusa district. The members visit Urakannon-do, a lesser-known yet powerful good luck spot located behind the main hall of Sensoji Temple. Following their visit to the power spot, they discover Kadoura Coffee, a quaint cafe known for its "secret menu." Here, the idols enjoy a special French toast and try various unique beverages, including an Apple Honey Latte, Orange Espresso, and an Organic Coffee Banana Shake, sharing lighthearted moments and casual conversation.
