### Video Summary
In this video, RE-GE member Jui Nogimoto participates in a "100 Questions, 100 Answers" challenge to help fans get to know her better. ### RE-GE Official Information
RE-GE is a girl group formed through the "HAJIMARE Project," a collaboration between Stardust Promotion and Bandai Namco Music Live. They made their major debut on June 3, 2026, with the single "0-byo Mae."
