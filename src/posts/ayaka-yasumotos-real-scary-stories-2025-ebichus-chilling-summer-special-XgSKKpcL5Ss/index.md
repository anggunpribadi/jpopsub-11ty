**Ayaka Yasumoto’s Real Scary Stories 2025** is the latest installment in the popular annual series from the idol group Private Ebisu Junior High (Ebichu), featured on the "EbiChu☆Nanyanen" program. In this haunting episode released in September 2025, Ayaka Yasumoto, joined by fellow members Kokona Sakuragi and Yuna Nakamura, delivers chilling ghost stories collected from listeners—perfect for cooling down the summer heat. 

Known for its annual updates and creative storytelling, the series uniquely blends idol charisma with Japanese horror traditions. This episode continues to showcase Ebichu’s dynamic personalities and their knack for entertainment, all while invoking goosebumps and intrigue.
