Get a look at RE-GE's summer release "Hirari One Piece" as the group teams up with their agency senior, Haruna Sakamoto (former TEAM SHACHI), for a special choreography session! 

RE-GE (リージェ) is a rising girls' group born from the collaboration between Stardust Promotion and Bandai Namco Music Live. Formed through the HAJIMARE Project auditions, the nine-member unit debuted with their first single, "0秒前," and now embarks on their first summer, bringing fresh energy and youthful spirit to Japan’s idol scene.

This video highlights:
- Haruna Sakamoto’s involvement in choreography and her mentorship for RE-GE.
- Behind-the-scenes footage of the group’s dance rehearsal, including fun interactions, dance tips, and group dynamics.
- A glimpse into the preparation for a surprise performance of "Hirari One Piece" at a major Tokyo event.

Perfect for idol enthusiasts and fans of RE-GE, this episode is a must-watch for those interested in the creative process and teamwork it takes to bring dynamic performances to life!
