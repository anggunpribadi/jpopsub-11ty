Join us as we relaunch **Mugen Company Stapla**, an innovative collaboration project involving all 59 idols from the renowned **Star Planet** family! This captivating initiative, starting July 1, 2026, aims to unite Japanese entertainment and local businesses, unlocking endless possibilities in the realms of advertising, product development, collaborative song production, and unique unit formations. 

Hosted by **Sasaki Ayaka** and her co-host **Yuki Rina**—and joined by special guests **Suzuki Moeka**, **Hanakawa Yuno**, and **Tsukino**—this video offers a comprehensive overview of the refreshed Mugen Company Stapla project, a groundbreaking venture where idols act as company members equipped with unique talents to solve real-world challenges faced by businesses, organizations, and local shops across Japan.

The video details how these idols channel their individuality and creativity into meaningful collaborations, uplifting companies through tailor-made advertising campaigns, innovative product creation, and empowering corporate initiatives. The presenters also explore the project’s previous successes, including:
- **Momoiro Clover Z** partnering with **Nihon Yusei Group** to create the energizing "Mekimeki Exercise."
- Collaborative songs, such as "Words of Mutual Help," generated to celebrate major anniversaries.
- Partnerships with companies such as **PC Company** and **ArcRaising Research**.
- Live-streamed crowdfunding campaigns where idols showcased collaborative products and shared heartfelt stories behind their creation. 

As the name "Mugen Company" (Infinite Company) suggests, this initiative is designed to redefine the limits of entertainment, offering endless opportunities for cooperation and creative problem-solving. Through this project, idols don’t just perform—they step into the roles of passionate collaborators, entrepreneurs, and creators, actively participating in the challenges and goals of their partners.
