Discover the inspiring journey of MISS MERCY, a seven-member Japanese girl group, as they stand at the threshold of their major label debut. This documentary captures their vulnerable moments, heartfelt reactions, and unwavering determination during the announcement of their long-awaited major debut set for October 14, 2026. Through behind-the-scenes footage and emotional interviews, viewers witness the members’ excitement, gratitude, and reflection on years of effort, including intensive vocal and dance training since 2022.

MISS MERCY has already made waves through their debut single "Cinderella" and appearances at high-profile events like Tokyo Girls Collection. This episode gives fans an intimate look at the bonds between the members, their journey of growth, and their dreams as they embrace a new chapter in their careers. The documentary also highlights upcoming releases and live events, and showcases the group’s commitment to their supporters, team, and families.

Join MISS MERCY as they transform challenges into triumphs, sharing their most authentic selves before embarking on their major debut.
