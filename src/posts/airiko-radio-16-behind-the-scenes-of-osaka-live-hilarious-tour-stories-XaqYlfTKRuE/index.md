Welcome to Episode 16 of **Airiko Radio**, featuring Ai Kiyama and Riko Ueda from the Kyushu-based idol group **Batten Girls**! 

In this episode, the duo looks back at their recent live event  in Osaka held on August 11th, sharing behind-the-scenes stories and funny memories from the trip. Riko talks about being so excited and nervous (like a child before a school field trip) that she couldn't eat or sleep properly, even laughing out loud in her sleep on the Shinkansen ride back to Fukuoka. Meanwhile, Ai shares her perspective on Riko's unusual behavior backstage and how much fun they had performing with the comedians. 

Tune in to hear their relaxed and candid chat about recent events, lighthearted banter, and the unique chemistry between the two members!
