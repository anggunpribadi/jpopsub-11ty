Join Kazami Nonoka and Nakamura Yuna of Shiritsu Ebisu Chugaku (Ebichu) for an exciting episode of Ebichu's SHOWROOM! In this live broadcast, the members kick off by choosing an MC through a fun voting system. Get ready as they dive into a special "Enquete Talk" session, where audience input shapes the conversation.

The first major topic revealed is the upcoming release of a highly anticipated photobook, set for June 10th! The members share their excitement and some behind-the-scenes details, including their favorite shots and the concept behind the book. Kazami Nonoka discusses her photobook shot in Yamagata's Zao and Shizuoka's Shimoda, highlighting the contrasting scenery. Nakamura Yuna also touches upon her recent solo photobook, mentioning her initial desire to shoot in Okinawa before opting for Hokkaido due to weather concerns.
