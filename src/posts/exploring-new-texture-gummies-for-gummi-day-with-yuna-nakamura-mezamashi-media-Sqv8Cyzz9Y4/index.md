### Video Overview
In celebration of "Gummi Day" on September 3rd, Yuna Nakamura from Shiritsu Ebisu Chugaku introduces the latest and most innovative textured gummies on the popular Japanese morning segment *Mezamashi TV* ("Imadoki"). 

### Featured Products & Locations:
- **Gumi Zaurus (Nobel Confectionery):** Dinosaur-shaped gummies featuring a unique chewy-yet-crispy texture with a juicy grape flavor.
- **Dragon Gummies (PLAZA Tokyo Store):** Crunchy exterior with a soft, chewy blue raspberry center.
- **Gumi Tsuretata (Kracie):** An interactive DIY gummy-making kit updated to create longer, chewy gummy ropes.
- **Nectar Gumi:** Ultra-rich, fruit-concentrate gummies offering an intensely fruity flavor and a firm, satisfying bite.
- **Artisan Fruit Gummies (Patisserie Noriette, Setagaya):** Premium French-style artisanal gummies made without gelatin, utilizing concentrated fruit purees for a rich, melt-in-the-mouth texture available in five varieties (including mango).
