OCHA NORMA

It is a coined word that combines a relaxing place where everyone gathers (living room) and the Latin word (NORMA), which means "standard/criterion", and is pronounced "Ocha Norma".
We named them with the hope that they will become a new generation standard that entertains the living room audience!

In the coming era, we will utilize the diverse characters of our members to deliver performances and content that can be enjoyed by people of all ages and genders.
