### Video Summary
In this installment of the "100 Questions & Answers" series, RE-GE member Yumi Hiyama sits down to answer a rapid-fire round of personal questions. The video offers fans an intimate look at her personality, preferences, and daily habits. 

During the session, Yumi shares insights into her background—including her hometown of Aichi and her MBTI type (ENFJ)—as well as fun facts like her love for cats named Daizu and Mame, her unique dream of owning a pet crocodile, and her specific preferences regarding taiyaki (bean-filled fish cakes). This lighthearted interview provides viewers with a deeper connection to the group's members as they navigate their journey following their major debut with the single "0-byou Mae."


### About RE-GE
RE-GE is a girl group formed by Stardust Promotion and Bandai Namco Music Live. They debuted on June 3, 2026, following the "HAJIMARE Project" audition and their debut live performance at Tokyu Kabukicho Tower.
