Welcome to a special edition of the "Yakan to Idol" interview series! This episode brings together two charismatic idols who both share the nickname “Riko Riko”: Nakayama Riko of Shiritsu Ebisu Chugaku and Ueda Riko of Batten Shoujo Tai. For the first time, these two talented artists sit down for an intimate conversation, taking viewers behind the scenes of their lives, personalities, and fascinating similarities.

Even though they've crossed paths at live events before, this marks their first real opportunity to talk one-on-one. Watch as they navigate initial nerves, discover they’re the same age and share the same blood type, reminisce about their nicknames, and bond over their mutual love of dogs (both own adorable Toy Poodles!). The conversation unfolds into their everyday routines, favorite hobbies like shopping and relaxing at home, and funny moments such as oversleeping by accident.

If you're a fan of idol culture or of either Riko, this episode is a must-watch! You’ll get a rare peek at their authenticity, friendship, and the unique traits that connect and set them apart. Stay tuned for future parts as "Yakan-kun," the mascot kettle, brings you even more charming idol dialogues from Stardust Planet.
