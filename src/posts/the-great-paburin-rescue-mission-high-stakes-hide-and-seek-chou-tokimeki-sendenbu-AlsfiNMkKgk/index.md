### Video Summary
In this episode of the *Chou Tokimeki Sendenbu* variety series, the group faces a thrilling challenge: "The Great Paburin Rescue Mission." Their mascot, Paburin, has been captured and placed in the center of a wedding venue. 

To secure his release, the members must play a high-stakes, "can-kicking" style game of hide-and-seek. The rules require one member to act as the "Oni" (seeker) while the others attempt to navigate the venue and rescue Paburin without being tagged. To ensure the safety of the members during their tour, the game features a unique "no-running" rule, requiring contestants to move using only fast-walking. The video captures the members' chaotic, high-energy efforts to outsmart the seeker and bring their mascot back to safety.
